My favorite food is blueberry muffins with milk. It taste really good, especially straight out of the oven when it is nice and warm. I like to dip the bluebery muffin inside the milk just like a cookie. It is a little bit messy but I promise it is worth it. Next time you have a blueberry muffin try it with some milk.
