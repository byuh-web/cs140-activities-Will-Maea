Why Food?

Everybody loves food. It's one of those things that just make life better no matter where you're from. For me food isn't just something I eat when I'm hungry, it's comfort, cravings and good memories all mixed together.

Some foods remind me of home and family, while others are just my go to favourites when I want something good to eat. This website is a little collection of the foods I love and why I keep going back to them
