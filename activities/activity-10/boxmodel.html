What is normal flow in HTML?

Normal flow in HTML is the default way the browser displays elements in the order they appear in the code, with block elements stacking vertically and inline elements flowing horizontally.

What does float do? Why do you need to clear the float?

Float moves an element to the left or right of its container so other content (like text) wraps around it, and you need to clear the float because floated elements are taken out of normal flow, which can cause parent elements to collapse or other elements to wrap around them unintentionally.
